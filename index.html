Aria — Privacy Policy

Aria is a personal assistant app used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

When the owner authorizes it, Aria accesses the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail to read events and messages and to create events or send email on the owner's request.

This data is processed on the owner's own computer, used only to provide the assistant's features to the owner, and is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps.
